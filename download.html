Download TileDropper

TileDropper is a single file. Save it, double-click it, and it opens in your browser, no internet needed after the first download.

Download TileDropper.html (1.7 MB)Or use it online

1. Download

Click the button. Your browser saves TileDropper.html to your downloads folder.

2. Open

Double-click the file, or drag it into Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

3. Create

Open a MIDI or MusicXML file and start styling. Your settings and saved looks stay in that browser.

Good to know

  • Works offline for everything except the web fonts for titles, which fall back to standard fonts.
  • Requirements: a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS.
  • YouTube upload needs an internet connection and your own Google client ID, and works best from the online version.
  • Free to use for personal and commercial videos. Piano samples are by Alexander Holm under CC BY 3.0, so credit them if you redistribute the samples themselves.
TileDropper is free to use. Your files stay in your browser; nothing is uploaded unless you choose to send a finished video to YouTube.
